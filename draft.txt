Company Overview – MS&AD Insurance Group Holdings, Inc.

MS&AD Insurance Group Holdings, Inc. is a leading Japanese insurance group formed in 2010 through the consolidation of Aioi Insurance, Nissay Dowa General Insurance, and Mitsui Sumitomo Insurance Group. The Group remains one of the largest P&C and life insurance platforms in Asia, reporting ¥6.66T in ordinary income and ¥691.6B in net income for FY2024 (FYE March 2024) based on the latest disclosed fact sheet .

The Group operates through a multi-brand structure—primarily Mitsui Sumitomo Insurance, Aioi Nissay Dowa Insurance, MSI Aioi Life, and MSI Primary Life—providing a diversified product suite across auto, fire, casualty, commercial P&C, protection-type life, annuity, and savings products. This structure supports stable recurring premium inflows across both retail and corporate segments.

MS&AD maintains a broad international footprint with operations in 46 countries, enhancing its earnings diversification through Lloyd’s, specialty, and reinsurance platforms under MS Amlin, MS Amlin AG, MS Amlin Underwriting, and, more recently, Transverse Insurance Group. The Group also retains strong competitive positioning in key Asian markets, including #1 non-life market share in ASEAN, supporting its international expansion strategy.

Business Segment Composition (FY2024):
The Group’s earnings are sourced from four well-defined business fields:

Domestic Non-Life Insurance (~57%) – Core earnings driver with stable premium flows from auto and commercial lines.

Overseas Insurance (~23%) – Specialty, Lloyd’s, MGA-fronting, and reinsurance activities providing geographic and product diversification.

Domestic Life Insurance (~15%) – Steady contribution from protection and savings-type products across two life subsidiaries.

Financial & Risk-Related Services (~5%) – Earnings from asset management, risk consulting, and ancillary financial services.
This mix provides balanced exposure across domestic retail, corporate, and international specialty markets.


The acquisition of Transverse Insurance Group in 2023 strengthened MS&AD’s U.S. presence and expanded its access to delegated underwriting, hybrid-fronting, and capital-light specialty segments. The subsidiary’s footprint across New York, New Jersey, and Texas supports MS&AD’s strategy to scale fee-based and reinsurance-linked earnings.

Financial performance in FY2024 reflected improved underwriting conditions, lower catastrophe losses, and stronger investment income, supporting a 16.3% ROE and reinforcing the Group’s capital generation capacity. The Group continues to prioritize disciplined underwriting, capital efficiency, and digital transformation to sustain profitability amid competitive domestic markets and global regulatory changes.
